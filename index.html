My Teaching Philosophy

Goals and classroom practice for Grades 5–10, grounded in Nepal’s school curriculum and focused on Nepali and Social Studies.

Curriculum-Based Learning

I plan lessons around grade-level learning goals, school resources, and students’ interests.

  • Connect Nepali language, reading, writing, and communication skills with meaningful practice.
  • Explore Social Studies through Nepal’s communities, geography, history, culture, and civic life.
  • Relate key ideas to familiar local examples and everyday situations.

Inclusive, Student-Centered Teaching

I consider students’ different strengths, experiences, language backgrounds, and learning needs.

  • Balance whole-class explanation with pair work, group activities, and individual support.
  • Use clear examples, visual materials, discussion, reading, and guided practice.
  • Encourage every learner to participate, ask questions, and build confidence.

Assessment That Guides Learning

I use regular checks for understanding to recognise progress and decide what to teach next.

  • Use questions, classwork, short quizzes, reading or writing tasks, and unit assessments.
  • Give constructive feedback and time to practise or improve.
  • Revisit a topic in a new way when students need more support.

Active and Relevant Learning

Students understand more deeply when they discuss ideas and connect learning to the world around them.

  • Use maps, timelines, local observations, stories, and group projects in Social Studies.
  • Build Nepali skills through reading, vocabulary, writing, speaking, and creative activities.
  • Promote critical thinking, cooperation, and responsible participation.

Practice and Progress

Consistent practice helps students strengthen foundations and prepare for grade-level assessments.

  • Break challenging topics into clear steps, followed by guided and independent work.
  • Use revision, assignments, and practice questions to reinforce understanding.
  • Help students reflect on feedback, recognise growth, and set learning goals.

Respectful Classroom Community

A safe, organised, and encouraging classroom gives students room to learn and grow.

  • Model respect for Nepal’s diverse cultures, communities, and perspectives.
  • Set clear expectations and encourage responsibility, kindness, and teamwork.
  • Communicate with families and colleagues to support student progress.
